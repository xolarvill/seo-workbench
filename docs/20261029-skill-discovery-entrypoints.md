Date: 2026-10-29 (Asia/Shanghai). Approved plan step 9: improve discovery of research, writing, editing, technical, conversion, distribution and growth methods.

## Problem and change

The previous index linked all 60 canonical skills, but gave detailed task routing mainly to core SEO methods. Other capabilities appeared as grouped names without comparable trigger, input, output or ownership guidance. Flattening directories had removed source-folder nesting without fully resolving selection cost.

[The rewritten index](../skills/README.md) provides a short task locator and nine capability tables. Every canonical skill has one row describing its trigger, useful inputs, expected output and optional combinations. Inputs are evidence to look for, not mandatory questionnaires; outputs follow the user's requested scope. The index remains navigation rather than a scheduler or an allowlist.

Adjacent methods now have explicit divisions:

- SEO scope: site diagnosis, single-page diagnosis and technical-only investigation.
- Content: usefulness, credibility, expression, article refresh, marketing copy and approved-draft delivery.
- Architecture: seed-topic hub/spoke planning versus SERP-overlap clustering of a keyword set.
- Comparison pages: buyer/offer framing versus search structure, sharing evidence and scope.
- Conversion: page persuasion, lead/checkout form, popup, registration, post-signup activation and in-product upgrade. Public pricing-page persuasion is separate from pricing/packaging decisions.
- Evidence: provider operation selection versus domain interpretation; backlink analysis versus acquisition planning.

Reader-experience-review remains the single expression method during composition and revision. Combinations do not add mandatory style passes. Existing decisions, research and evidence remain the starting point when resuming work. Sources and license attribution are linked from a maintenance section rather than used as the capability taxonomy.
