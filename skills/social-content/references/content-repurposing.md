# Content repurposing

Read when an existing article needs platform-specific derivative drafts. Use [social-content](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Read the source article, its strongest supported claims, intended audience and usable attribution.
2. Choose destination formats that serve the requested audience; adapt the explanation rather than copying the introduction.
3. Preserve factual meaning and identify where the source or original author should be linked.
4. Review each version under the current platform and writing methods; preparing variants does not send or publish them.

## Deliverable

Deliver channel-specific drafts and source attribution, with publication left to the requested project workflow.
