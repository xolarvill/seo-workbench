# Prose pattern review

The historical filename is retained for source comparison. Use [reader-experience-review](../../../reader-experience-review/SKILL.md) for decisions and [the shared editing reference](../../../write-content/references/anti-slop-ruleset.md) when a passage needs closer attention.

## Read the passage first

Identify its reader, task and intended meaning. A useful technical explanation, safety instruction or concise list may need no changes. Sentence rhythm, punctuation and a vocabulary hit cannot establish AI authorship or poor quality.

| Candidate passage | What to inspect | Useful edit when warranted |
|---|---|---|
| Generic introduction | Does it delay the answer? | Start with the supported answer or action |
| Abstract praise such as “seamless” | Is there an observable benefit behind the adjective? | State that benefit from actual product evidence |
| “It's worth noting” | Does the following condition matter here? | State the condition directly, or remove the detour |
| Repeated denials or qualifications | Which reader choice changes? Has the scope already been given? | Keep the needed condition once, at the useful location |
| Contrast with “most guides” or “some people” | Is the compared claim actually introduced or evidenced? | Explain the method directly when there is no real comparison |
| Dense clauses or repeated transitions | Can the reader follow the relationship? | Split or connect the specific ideas |

## Preserve useful language

Technical terms such as “robust standard errors” keep their established meaning. Negation may express a vital condition. Three parallel steps can be the clearest structure. Dashes and sentence lengths need no quotas. Choose punctuation and wording for the actual sentence and the article's language.

Use facts, supplied experience and appropriate examples when they improve explanation. Do not add first-person testing, deliberate imperfections, stories or register shifts to manufacture authenticity. For an authorized edit, preserve sources and factual distinctions and read the result in context. For a review, distinguish required edits, useful refinements and accepted passages.
