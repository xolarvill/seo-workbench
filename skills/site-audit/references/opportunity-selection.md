# Audit opportunity selection

Use for a site-wide audit that must choose what to improve first. Read [site-audit](../SKILL.md) for scope.

Build a bounded shortlist across the opportunity types the evidence supports: existing underperformance, unmet useful demand, winners to protect, eligible technical blockers and next-step friction. Do not require every category or a fixed row count.

Compare stronger and weaker/typical pages within meaningful families when it could change the recommendation. Read main content rather than navigation or keyword rows alone. Protect effective answers; a high rank does not forbid correcting factual or usability problems.

For each serious candidate record page/family, observed problem, evidence source/date/market, customer job, proposed change, plausible mechanism, effort and main uncertainty. Separate measured first-party observations from provider estimates. A crawler warning, high volume, low effort or hypothetical first-place traffic is not sufficient by itself to lead.

Choose the actions most directly supported by business fit and diagnosis. Explain why the strongest rejected option loses, and record other material findings as deferred, maintenance, unsupported or out of scope with reasons. Stop research when another lookup is unlikely to change the choice, subject to required evidence eligibility.

State benefits directionally when baseline or demand is missing. Search volume is not visits; existing traffic is not wholly incremental headroom; close-variant buckets overlap. Hypothetical scenarios need explicit assumptions and cannot invent conversion/revenue.

Review whether the leading action survives the strongest alternative and whether every material finding has a disposition. Use the existing project report and shared expression method, not upstream HTML or fixed bullet rules.
