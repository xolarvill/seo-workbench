# Article editing review

Read when an article needs a substantive editing pass after research and drafting. Use [copy-editing](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Read the brief, intended reader and actual draft together. Compare the opening promise with the explanation and conclusion.
2. Review section order, transitions, paragraph focus, examples and technical explanations. Locate passages whose specificity or supporting evidence is missing.
3. Distinguish structural edits from line edits. Preserve documented facts, attribution, numbers and the intended brand voice.
4. Use reader-experience-review as the single expression method for the revised passage; use this reference only to identify relevant article-level inputs.
5. Select only the editing sweeps relevant to this article. A clear technical or maintenance passage may need zero changes. Do not add a story, emotional stakes, contrarian opening or hypothetical objection merely to make information seem persuasive. Retain genuine reader questions, useful comparisons and evidence-based cautions when they help the task.

## Deliverable

For a requested audit, return passage locations, the reader problem, proposed edits and essential factual inputs. For authorized direct editing, return the requested edited text and keep diagnostic notes in the existing task artifact. Keep proposed structural changes distinguishable from applied edits.
