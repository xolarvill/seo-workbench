# Page-level link prospecting

Use when the request needs realistic acquisition targets rather than a general strategy. Establish the asset, audience, market and the editorial reason to reference it.

Discover prospects from relevant observed SERPs, curated resources, actual competitor links, adjacent vendors and local associations when appropriate. Keep product competitors, publishers and communities distinguishable; do not discard a useful source merely because it is a forum or has a tracking parameter.

Read each leading target page. Record its URL, current relevant content/link, observed date, source, exact inclusion/correction/resource angle, topical fit, public contact path and the URL where that contact was found. Never generate an email address or claim a provider found contact data returned only by web reading.

Prefer a specific editorial fit over a high domain metric. Distinguish confirmed targets from unverified leads and likely paid placements. Missing backlink records cannot prove no links; domain metrics cannot establish quality or willingness to link.

Deliver a prioritized prospect table and the requested personalized draft angles. If no contact is found, give the next concrete discovery path. Drafting does not authorize sending, paid placements or mass outreach.
