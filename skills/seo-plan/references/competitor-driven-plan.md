# Competitor-driven starting plan

Use for a new site or a roadmap informed by competitors. Read [seo-plan](../SKILL.md) and relevant existing project decisions.

Use [competitive-landscape](../../competitive-landscape/SKILL.md) to identify search/business competitors and comparable evidence. When possible include a market leader and a relevant smaller site; size alone does not establish attainability. Use [reverse-engineer-seo](../../reverse-engineer-seo/SKILL.md) only when a single site's acquisition pattern needs deeper explanation.

Feed supported opportunities into [keyword-research](../../keyword-research/SKILL.md); use existing inventory and intended page owners to distinguish retain, strengthen and create. Plan query/page assignments, required expertise/data, dependencies and resources, rather than copying the competitor's publishing volume.

Use [linkbuilding](../../linkbuilding/SKILL.md) for relevant link themes and assets. Provider counts and sampled links stay source-scoped; do not discard an editorially useful source based on a generic site-type blacklist.

Deliver a prioritized first-page / next-page plan with business rationale, evidence, effort, uncertainties and review dates. Explain what to skip for now. Research results are not permission to start trackers, recurring collection, publishing or redirects.

## SaaS commercial pages

When the product is SaaS, examine distinct buyer jobs: alternatives, head-to-head comparisons, competitor pricing and category-for-audience/use-case. Check current pages and actual SERP formats before recommending new URLs. Existing useful pages may need correction or strengthening instead.

Distinguish competitor-branded searches from the brand's own alternatives searches; the latter may reveal reputation or retention questions rather than a page to create. Check vendor facts/prices at their owning sources when a comparison is requested.

Volume and CPC are market context. Search volume multiplied by CPC is neither advertising spend nor predicted organic value; it lacks click share and conversion economics. Do not use it as revenue or sole priority. Unknown values and low-volume high-fit jobs stay visible.

After choosing a page, hand buyer framing to [competitor-alternatives](../../competitor-alternatives/SKILL.md), search structure to [seo-competitor-pages](../../seo-competitor-pages/SKILL.md), and drafting to the appropriate writing skill. Share one research artifact rather than duplicate briefs.
