# Trend research

Read when a planning question concerns a recent rise in topic interest. Use [content-strategy](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Compare explicit, comparable windows and use rates when window lengths differ.
2. Inspect the queries and pages behind the change, market relevance and finalized coverage.
3. Separate recurring seasonality, branded demand and a new topic hypothesis where evidence supports that distinction.
4. Assess whether existing pages can answer the demand and whether additional research is worth doing.

## Deliverable

Provide observed changes, source windows, relevance, confidence and a proportionate content response.
