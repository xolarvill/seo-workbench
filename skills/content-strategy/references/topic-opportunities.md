# Topic opportunity review

Read when content planning needs a view of strong and weak topic coverage. Use [content-strategy](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Map current pages and observed query coverage to reader tasks and business-relevant topic groups.
2. Review coverage breadth, useful supporting pages and their existing performance.
3. Differentiate a thin topic group from a group that is simply unobserved in the available evidence.
4. Propose reinforcement, maintenance or expansion based on the actual gap and delivery capacity.

## Deliverable

Return topic groups, observed supporting pages, gaps and proposed priorities with evidence limits.
