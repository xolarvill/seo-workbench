# AIO and Featured Snippet Decision Framework

Choose the work from the reader's question, current evidence and authorized objective. A featured snippet, AI Overview, ordinary ranking and click outcome are distinct observations. Do not assume that one suppresses the other or that a query category guarantees a feature.

## 1. Observe before choosing a feature target

Reuse applicable recent observations or collect a bounded current sample when the SERP may have changed. Record query, date, market, language, device and observed result URLs. Identify featured snippets, AI Overviews, PAA and ordinary results independently. Note unavailable observations rather than interpreting them as absence.

Do not extrapolate vendor prevalence or click correlations to the current site. A reported correlation does not establish that obtaining a citation will increase this site's clicks.

## 2. Match the work to observations

| Observed situation | Useful next work |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Featured snippet present | Review the answer's factual accuracy, clarity and format against the reader's task; improve only concrete gaps |
| AI Overview present | Inspect cited claims and source support; identify useful evidence or explanation missing from the page |
| Both present | Evaluate both observations independently; keep one coherent reader-focused answer |
| Neither observed | Improve the underlying answer and search intent fit when needed; do not manufacture FAQs to target a predicted feature |

A definition may need prose, a procedure steps and a comparison a table. There is no universal answer length, ranking cutoff or guaranteed schema requirement for citation.

## 3. Keep markup and content separate

Use [structured data guidance](structured-data-snippets.md) and the [shared policy](../../schema/references/search-eligibility.md). Google FAQ and HowTo rich results are retired; FAQPage does not have a demonstrated general AI citation uplift here. Do not prescribe FAQ or HowTo markup as an AIO recipe.

Name genuine entities, cite material claims and clarify dates, versions and limits when relevant. Use author attribution where it represents actual work, without treating a byline or pronoun as an authority score. For measurement, use the [generative search observation method](../../write-content/references/geo-optimization.md).

## 4. Assess value and measure change

Consult [zero-click and AIO impact](../../keyword-deep-dive/references/zero-click-and-aio-impact.md) when estimating traffic opportunity, while preserving its data and scope limits. A modeled traffic estimate is not observed site performance.

Record a baseline before a substantive edit, then compare suitable query/market/device observations and available site outcomes. Choose follow-up timing based on indexing, collection availability and the decision needed, not a guaranteed reprocessing deadline. Avoid repeated edits that prevent an interpretable comparison.

## Deliverable

Record the observed features, reader problem, proposed change, evidence pointers and follow-up plan. Keep content review, implementation, indexing, appearance and business results distinct. If the existing answer works, no rewrite is required.
