# Content performance review

Read when content work needs prioritization from observed performance. Use [drift](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Read comparable GSC, GA4 and business artifacts with their exact windows, filters and regimes.
2. Identify page-level patterns in visibility, clicks, observed engagement and relevant business context.
3. Distinguish strong pages, declining pages, possible CTR opportunities and pages needing a separate diagnosis.
4. Check available coverage and uncertainty before prioritizing a change. Use existing Portfolio diagnostics and controls instead of inventing an ROI score.

## Deliverable

Return a prioritized review queue with evidence pointers, metric definitions, proposed work and a follow-up window.
