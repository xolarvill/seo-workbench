# Topic research handoff

Read when a topic needs research before a brief is prepared. Use [keyword-deep-dive](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Confirm the topic, market, reader task and any existing page owner.
2. Inspect applicable SERP observations, competitor pages and relevant audience questions.
3. Summarize format, intent, entities, coverage and a defensible differentiating contribution.
4. Identify the source facts, examples and first-party input a writer would need, then pass the research to content-brief.

## Deliverable

Deliver a research summary with observed sources, ownership decision, proposed scope and unresolved inputs.
