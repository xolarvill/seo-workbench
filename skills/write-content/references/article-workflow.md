# Research-to-article handoff

Read when an article requires coordinating research, a brief and a draft. Use [write-content](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Locate existing approved research and the brief, confirming their target, market and evidence scope.
2. Fill only material research gaps, including audience/community evidence when the task requires it.
3. Map the main contribution and supporting sources to the article sections.
4. Draft using the canonical writing method, then use the existing review and project handoff conventions.

## Deliverable

Deliver the draft and its supporting task record, preserving factual gaps outside the reader-facing article unless needed for meaning.
