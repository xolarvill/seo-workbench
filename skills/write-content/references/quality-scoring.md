# Technique 16: Content Quality Review

## Purpose and scope

Use this reference for task completeness, factual support and applicable SEO acceptance. [The reader method](../../reader-experience-review/SKILL.md) alone decides expression; this reference is not another prose review.

Review whether content fulfills its brief and reader task using the evidence available. Use findings by dimension rather than a composite score. A numeric total cannot establish factual accuracy, publication approval, AI authorship or expected search performance.

Set the review scope first: a three-sentence instruction, section draft and full SEO article need different checks. Mark checks outside that scope as not applicable. Missing observations are not zero-quality findings.

## Review dimensions

### Reader task and completeness

- Does the passage answer the promised question or support the intended action?
- Are necessary prerequisites, steps, conditions and limits clear?
- Does the opening promise match the content?
- For a full article, compare coverage with the approved brief and applicable research. Do not add every competitor topic if it distracts from the reader's task.

### Accuracy and attribution

- Are material facts, dates, numbers and instructions supported by the identified sources?
- Are manufacturer guidance, reported research, author testing and editorial inference distinguishable?
- Are genuine quotations, credentials, case studies and first-party results attributed accurately?
- Are unresolved claims removed, narrowed or flagged? Do not replace missing evidence with first-person wording.

### Useful depth and information gain

- Does the explanation provide enough detail for this reader to act or decide?
- Where the brief promises original research, comparison or experience, is that promise supported?
- Does synthesis clarify conditions, reconcile sources or expose a useful gap?
- Keep useful common facts and standard procedures. Novelty is not required in every section; do not add contrarian views or examples just to earn credit.

### Reader-method findings

Use any relevant findings from the reader method already applied during composition. Do not rescore voice, create a second style checklist or request changes to clear a stylistic gate. A factual acceptance issue identifies the claim to correct; use [evidence to prose](../../reader-experience-review/references/evidence-to-prose.md) to express the supported result.

### SEO and discoverability, when in scope

- Does the page address the intended search intent with natural terminology?
- Are title, H1 and description accurate and useful rather than formulaic?
- Are internal links relevant, verified and descriptive? There is no density quota.
- Are sources linked where verification helps, without padding citations?
- Does proposed structured data match visible content and current implementation requirements?
- Are images, URLs and mobile structure appropriate for the page?

A local prose review does not require metadata, schema, a FAQ or a new SERP study. Answer-first formatting should fit the actual answer, without a prescribed word count.

## Findings and revision decisions

Use a status such as **satisfied**, **needs change**, **not verified** or **not applicable**, with evidence and a concrete reader consequence. Distinguish factual blockers from optional improvements.

Revise only identified problems, then check the affected facts and surrounding flow. A material unsupported instruction may block finalization even if every other dimension is satisfied. Several inapplicable SEO checks do not justify rewriting useful prose. Stop when required issues are resolved; do not force revision cycles to reach a score.

If necessary evidence or a user decision is missing, state exactly which claim or choice depends on it and continue independent work. An existing approved brief remains valid unless a material conflict changes it.
