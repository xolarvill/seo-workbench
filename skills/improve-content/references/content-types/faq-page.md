# Technique 21: FAQ Pages

## Purpose and when to use

Use a FAQ page or section to resolve genuine recurring questions about a topic, product or service. Prefer existing query evidence, support records, documented product guidance or research. Do not invent customer conversations, question frequency or usage experience.

Use a separate article when a question needs a substantial explanation; summarize and link when that helps navigation. An existing accurate FAQ page can remain useful without a Google FAQ rich result.

## Structure and scope

1. State what the page covers and who the answers help.
2. Group related questions by reader task when grouping makes navigation easier.
3. Give the direct answer first, followed by needed conditions, exceptions or evidence.
4. Link to authoritative instructions or a relevant deeper page where useful.
5. Offer a contact or support route when unanswered questions genuinely need one.

There is no required question count, word count or answer length. A one-sentence answer may be complete; a complex answer may need steps or a table. Use the question wording readers understand rather than forcing exact search phrases.

## Evidence and editorial review

- Verify product facts, pricing, eligibility and policy terms against the applicable source; include date, currency, units or version when material.
- Attribute manufacturer instructions, research, actual support observations and first-party experience accurately.
- Keep customer data private and use only authorized aggregate support evidence.
- Do not claim “our most common question” without frequency evidence.
- Review factual changes and obsolete answers when policies or products change; do not refresh dates without reviewing substance.

## Structured data and search appearance

Use the [schema skill](../../../schema/SKILL.md) and [shared eligibility reference](../../../schema/references/search-eligibility.md).

Google FAQ rich results stopped appearing for **all sites on 2026-05-07**. The former government/health exception no longer applies. FAQPage can describe truthful Q&A content as a semantic choice; it is not a new Google appearance opportunity or a demonstrated AI citation tactic.

Existing accurate FAQPage is not a critical defect solely because the appearance retired. Check visible content, actual consumers and maintenance needs before proposing changes. Do not automatically remove it or convert editorial FAQs into user-submitted Q&A markup.

BreadcrumbList may describe genuine navigation, but Google's breadcrumb appearance is desktop-only; absence on mobile does not prove a defect.

## Answer formatting and links

Where relevant search evidence shows a snippet or related question, use the format that accurately answers it. No formatting choice guarantees a snippet or citation. Link contextually to detailed instructions, definitions, comparisons or product terms without a link quota or repeated sales CTAs after every category.

## Completion review

Check that questions are relevant, answers correct, conditions clear and sources traceable. Record any missing facts. Preserve useful concise answers and report concrete edits rather than padding the page, adding personality or satisfying schema and density checklists.
