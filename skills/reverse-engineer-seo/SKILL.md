# Reverse-engineer a site's SEO

Explain what a single site appears to do successfully and what another site can realistically learn. A named site is enough; the user's own site is optional when tailoring recommendations.

Read the selected project's market and existing research. Use bounded public page observations and verified provider exports/operations; no competitor project creation, full crawl or paid operation is implied. Read [provider selection](../seo-dataforseo/SKILL.md) and [backlinks](../backlinks/SKILL.md) when those sources are relevant. Without provider data, do a qualitative teardown with explicit limits instead of manufacturing traffic or authority.

1. Orient with available domain, ranking and backlink observations. Record provider, market, result type, observation date, retrieval date, ranking depth, limits and completeness. Provider traffic estimates are not measured visits.
2. Separate branded and non-branded queries using actual brand/product aliases. For sampled rows, label every share with its sampled denominator. A top-100 traffic estimate share is not the site's total brand traffic share.
3. Group queries by observed URL and meaningful page family, preserving locale/subdomain scope. Identify repeated customer jobs and query patterns. Use paginated data or targeted family queries where a leading recommendation needs coverage beyond the sample. Ranking-keyword share is not visit share; totals from overlapping families cannot be added.
4. Read representative high-value pages before inferring how they are made: authored guides, comparison pages, tools, templates, catalog or data-driven pages. If blocked, title/snippet observations cannot establish the full template or content.
5. Group actually observed linking pages by target asset and editorial reason. Verify the pages behind decisive claims. A sampled high-metric link list is neither a complete link profile nor a causal explanation of ranking. Provider absence stays unknown; use source-scoped counts.
6. Propose transferable moves: page type, search pattern, differentiating value, evidence, feasible first page, effort and uncertainty. Explain what depends on brand demand, proprietary data, scale, existing links or resources the user's site does not have.

Deliver a page-family/query-pattern table, link-asset observations and a reasoned shortlist in the existing project report. Include source coverage and unread pages. Do not copy competitor text or imply measured outcomes from estimated metrics.

Hand chosen keyword opportunities to [keyword-research](../keyword-research/SKILL.md), cross-market questions to [competitive-landscape](../competitive-landscape/SKILL.md), and the user's implementation roadmap to [seo-plan](../seo-plan/SKILL.md). A teardown alone does not select targets, create trackers or mutate the site.
