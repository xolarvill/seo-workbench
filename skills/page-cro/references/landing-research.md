# Landing-page research brief

Read when a landing page needs an intent and conversion brief before copy is drafted. Use [page-cro](../SKILL.md) as the canonical entrypoint.

## Method

1. Identify SEO or paid acquisition context, audience, offer and conversion goal.
2. Use actual query/ad and inspected competitor evidence to review message match and page expectations.
3. List the factual benefits, proof and decision conditions the page can support.
4. Hand off a proposed structure, CTA path, evidence needs and measurement plan to copywriting.

## Deliverable

Return a landing-page brief with sources, message hierarchy, proof gaps and the requested next deliverable.
